# Campus Placement Assistant — Placement Overview

> **Dataset status:** Synthetic/sample data created for a college project prototype. The company names, eligibility criteria, roles, skills, and selection rounds in this knowledge base are **not real or verified recruitment requirements**.

## Purpose

The Campus Placement Assistant helps students understand sample campus placement opportunities, eligibility requirements, job roles, required skills, selection rounds, and preparation areas.

## Information available

The knowledge base can be used to:
- Check sample company eligibility using branch, CGPA, and current backlogs.
- Explain sample job roles.
- Identify skills listed for a sample role or company.
- Explain sample selection rounds.
- Provide interview and placement preparation guidance.

## Eligibility inputs

For a sample eligibility check, the required student inputs are:
1. Branch
2. CGPA
3. Current number of backlogs

If any of these inputs is missing, ask the student for the missing information before giving a final eligibility result.

## Data limitations

This knowledge base contains synthetic demonstration data. It does not provide:
- Real company recruitment requirements
- Current job openings
- Salary information
- Application deadlines
- Actual interview dates
- Official college placement policies

If requested information is not present in the knowledge base, say that the information is not available in the provided knowledge sources. Do not invent it.

## Important terminology

Use **sample-eligible** or **sample criteria** when referring to the synthetic company data. Do not describe these sample records as official company eligibility.
